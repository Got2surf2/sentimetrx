## 2. THE PROBLEM

**$5.8B is spent annually on survey tools — and response quality is declining.**

- Average survey response rate has dropped to **5-15%** (SurveyMonkey, 2025)
- **80% of open-ended responses** are too vague to act on — "it was fine," "good," "OK"
- Companies use **3-5 separate tools** for surveys, email outreach, text analytics, translation, and reporting
- Enterprise text analytics platforms cost **$50K-500K/year** and require data science teams to operate
- **68% of consumers** prefer to give feedback in their native language but most surveys are English-only (CSA Research)

The result: organizations collect mountains of feedback data and extract almost no actionable insight from it.

---

## 3. THE INSIGHT

**The problem isn't that people don't want to give feedback. It's that surveys are a monologue pretending to be a conversation.**

When a respondent says "the wait was too long," a traditional survey moves to the next checkbox. A human interviewer would say "tell me more about that — how long did you wait?"

That follow-up is where the insight lives. And until now, it required a human.

---

## 4. THE SOLUTION

**SentimetRx replaces static surveys with AI-powered conversations that adapt in real-time.**

A branded AI bot greets respondents by name, asks questions conversationally, detects when answers are vague and asks intelligent follow-ups, handles off-topic questions gracefully, and works in 15 languages — all without any human in the loop.

Then it automatically extracts themes, tests for statistical significance, and generates boardroom-ready reports.

**One platform. Survey + outreach + analytics + reporting.**

## 5. PRODUCT

**Build in minutes. Converse at scale. Analyze instantly.**

**AI Study Wizard** — Select your industry and goals. AI generates a complete study with questions, psychographic measures, clarifier logic, and a branded bot persona. 7 blueprints, 18 industries.

**Conversational Collection** — Respondents chat with your bot on any device. AI clarifiers probe short answers. Smart deflection handles off-topic questions. Adaptive follow-ups ask different questions based on sentiment. 15 question types including skip logic.

**Built-in Campaigns** — Rich email templates (images, numbered steps, signatures), merge tag personalization, multi-sequence reminders, real-time open/click/completion tracking. No Mailchimp required.

**15-Language Support** — One-click AI translation of the entire study. Respondents choose their language. Responses auto-translated to English for unified analysis. No translation vendors.

**AI Analytics** — Theme extraction across thousands of responses. Chi-square significance testing. Segment comparison by sentiment, demographics, psychographics. Export as interactive HTML, PowerPoint, or CSV.

## 6. THE CONVERSATIONAL ADVANTAGE

**Same respondent. Same question. Dramatically different data quality.**

**Traditional survey:**
> How was your experience? ★★★★ (4/5)
> Any comments? "It was good"
>
> *Insight captured: positive sentiment. That's it.*

**SentimetRx conversation:**
> Bot: How was your experience? [emoji scale]
> Respondent: [taps Good — 4 stars]
> Bot: That's great to hear! What made it good?
> Respondent: The staff was really friendly but we waited a long time
> Bot: Could you tell me more about the wait?
> Respondent: 45 minutes for a table with a reservation on a Tuesday night
>
> *Insight captured: positive overall + staff friendliness driver + 45-min wait issue + reservation system problem + weekday context*

**3-5x more actionable text per respondent. Same survey length. No interviewer required.**
