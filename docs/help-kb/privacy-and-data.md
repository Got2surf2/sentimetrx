# Where does my data live and how is it protected?

Sentimetrx is built so each organization's data stays separate and under its
control. The full details are in the **Privacy Notice**, linked from every
survey, agent, and live-session page — this is a plain-language summary.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored on established cloud infrastructure in the **United States**,
and it's backed up for disaster recovery. Each organization's data is **isolated
from every other customer's**, so your responses and studies aren't mixed with
anyone else's.

## How it's used

Responses are analyzed — including with the help of AI services — to produce the
themes, sentiment, and summaries you see in Advanced Analytics, on behalf of the
organization that collected them. Your responses are **never sold** and are
**not used for advertising**.

Depending on the features you use, data may be processed by service providers for
things like AI analysis, transcription, hosting, storage, email, and error
monitoring. The Privacy Notice lists these providers by name.

## Who controls feedback

When someone answers a survey or chats with an agent, the **organization that
invited them controls that feedback** — it decides why it's collected, who sees
it, and how long it's kept. If an organization's workspace is deleted, its data,
including those responses, is erased.

## Getting more detail or making a request

For the complete picture — retention, the list of service providers, and how to
ask about, correct, or delete data — read the **Privacy Notice**. It also
includes the contact address for privacy questions.
