# How do I create an agent?

An **agent** is a branded, conversational AI chatbot — a bot, virtual assistant,
or FAQ concierge — you can put in front of your audience. Unlike a fixed survey, an agent holds a real back-and-forth
conversation — answering questions, probing for detail, and staying on-brand.
Once published, it's public at its own link and can be embedded on your site.

## Build an agent

Open **Agents** and create a new one. In the builder you set:

- **Identity** — the agent's name, emoji, personality, and opening message.
- **System prompt** — the standing instructions that shape how it answers.
- **Intents** — actions to nudge toward (donate, volunteer, register, or your
  own), triggered by keyword or AI detection to a link or message.
- **Guardrails** — banned topics and toggles for things like persona inference.

When it looks right, publish it, then share the chat link or embed it as an
iframe. Every agent carries a "Powered by Datanautix" badge.

## Give your agent knowledge

An agent answers from a **knowledge base** you build in the builder. You can:

- **Crawl your website** — point it at your site and it reads the pages into
  knowledge.
- **Upload documents** — add PDFs or docs.
- **Paste knowledge text** — drop in any facts you want it to know.

The agent retrieves the most relevant pieces on each turn, so keep the base
focused and accurate. As real conversations come in, the **Question Log** shows
what people asked that the agent couldn't answer — you can write an answer once
and it feeds straight back into the knowledge base.

## What is a Super Agent?

A **Super Agent** is a higher-capability tier you turn on per agent. Compared to
a standard agent, it:

- Runs on a **bigger model** (you pick it from a dropdown).
- Absorbs a **much larger knowledge base** and retrieves more deeply per answer.
- Can **actively search its own knowledge** mid-conversation and **check official
  pages** to ground an answer.
- Keeps its knowledge **fresh automatically** on a recurring refresh.

To enable it, edit the agent and open the **Agent Capability** section, choose the
**Super Agent** card, and pick a model. Use it when a plain FAQ concierge isn't
enough and you need an agent that can do heavier lifting.
