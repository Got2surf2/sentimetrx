# PulseIQ vs Town Hall — which do I use?

Both turn a group conversation — a **focus group**, panel, or town-hall meeting —
into insight, but they work very differently. **PulseIQ** runs a *live, digital*
session (a virtual focus group or live poll). **Town Hall** is for a *recorded,
in-person* meeting. Pick by whether people are typing into a link right now or
sitting in a room being recorded.

## PulseIQ — a live digital pulse

Use PulseIQ when you want to run a live poll or pulse and watch responses arrive
in real time. Participants join from a link (on their own phones or laptops) and
answer conversationally, while you follow along on the **Live Console** — a
facilitator view with a live feed, topic cards you can rotate through, and themes
that surface as people respond. You can moderate on the fly, mute or ban a
disruptive participant, and see sentiment and activity counts update as the
session runs.

**Use this when:** you're hosting a live, remote or in-the-room-but-on-devices
session and want answers streaming in as it happens.

## Town Hall — a recorded meeting, turned into a Q&A report

Use Town Hall when you have an in-person meeting (a presentation, a community
forum, a Q&A) that you want to *record* and turn into a clean, shareable report.
You set up the meeting (name, date, location, panel, objectives), attach the
audio or video, and Sentimetrx transcribes it. You review and correct the
transcript, name the speakers, then run the analysis to produce a neutral Q&A
summary, action items, and a Datanautix-branded PDF and deck. If a slide deck was
presented, it adds a "Meeting Overview" of what was shown.

**Use this when:** you recorded a meeting and want a polished Q&A write-up you can
hand to the organizer or client.

## The one-line difference

- **PulseIQ** = live, people type into a link, you watch it happen.
- **Town Hall** = recorded meeting, you get a report afterward.
