# Getting started — where do I click first?

Welcome to Sentimetrx. The idea is simple: **collect feedback**, then **turn it
into insight**. Here's the lay of the land and a suggested first path.

## The main areas

- **Surveys** — build chat-style questionnaires and share them by link or QR code.
- **Agents** — branded AI chatbots that hold a real conversation with your
  audience.
- **Advanced Analytics** (the Analyze module) — where responses become insight,
  with **TextMine** (themes, entities, dimensions, filtering), the **Statistics**
  tab (charts and key drivers), **Search**, and **Ask Ana**.
- **Campaigns** — send a survey by email or SMS and track opens and responses.
- **PulseIQ** and **Town Hall** — live digital pulse sessions and recorded
  in-person meetings, respectively.

## A good first path

1. **Get some feedback in.** Create a survey (try the Smart Study Wizard for a
   fast draft), or import data you already have — Google reviews, a CSV, and more.
2. **Collect responses.** Share your survey link or QR code, or send it out with
   Campaigns.
3. **Open Advanced Analytics.** Pick your dataset to explore it.
4. **Mine themes and dig in.** In TextMine, let Sentimetrx group open-ended
   answers into themes, then filter and compare. Switch to the Statistics tab for
   the numbers.
5. **Just ask.** Use **Ask Ana** inside the dataset to ask questions about your
   data in plain language ("what did detractors say about wait times?").

## Stuck on how to do something?

Click the **🛟 Help** button at the top of any page to chat with **Guide**, who
answers how-to questions about using Sentimetrx anytime. For questions about what
your data actually says, use **Ask Ana** inside a dataset instead.
