# How do I explore my results in TextMine?

**TextMine** is the heart of Analytics — it turns open-ended answers into
themes you can read, filter, and compare. Open a dataset in **Analytics**
to get there.

## Themes

Sentimetrx reads your open-ended responses and groups them into **themes** — the
recurring topics people bring up — and tags the exact verbatim quotes behind each
one. You can:

- **Add, rename, or remove** themes after mining to match how you think about the
  business.
- **Recount** themes without re-mining when you tweak them.
- See which segments a theme is more common in, with significance testing so you
  know a difference is real and not noise.

## Filtering and segmenting

Use the **Filters** bar to narrow the view — by rating or sentiment, demographics,
scores, dates, or response status — and combine conditions with AND/OR. Your
filters are saved in the page URL, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.
Charts, theme counts, and comparisons all update to match the active filter.

## Entities and Dimensions

Beyond themes, TextMine surfaces two more lenses:

- **Entities** — the specific things people name (dishes, drinks, places, people,
  brands). Great for "what exactly got mentioned?"
- **Dimensions** — a structured tagging of each comment across touchpoint,
  attribute, product, and more, including a severity flag for alerts. Emotion
  language (like disappointment or churn intent) shows here too, always with the
  quote as evidence.

## Word clouds, comparisons, and search

TextMine also gives you word clouds, side-by-side segment comparisons, and
full-text **Search** (plain or AI-augmented) to jump straight to the comments you
care about.

## Want an answer, not a chart?

If you'd rather just ask — "what did detractors say about wait times?" — use
**Ask Ana** inside the dataset. It reads the same data and answers in plain
language.
