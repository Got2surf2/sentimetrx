# How do I share my survey?

Once your survey is ready, you publish it and share a link — by QR code, a copied
URL, or an email/SMS campaign. Here's how.

## Publish it

From the survey creator, publish at the **Review** step. Publishing makes the
survey live so people can respond. You can pause or close it later from the
**Deploy** page whenever you want to stop collecting.

## Get the link and QR code

Open the **Deploy** page for your study. There you can:

- **Copy the survey link** to paste anywhere — a text, an email, your website, a
  social post.
- **Generate and download a QR code** to print on a table tent, receipt, poster,
  or flyer. People scan it to open the survey on their phone.
- **Control status** — activate, pause, or close the study.

The page also has embed instructions if you'd rather drop the survey into your own
site.

## One response per device, or multiple

In the survey's **Basics**, you choose how many times a single device can respond:

- **One response per device** — best for "one person, one answer" situations like a
  satisfaction survey.
- **Allow multiple responses** — best for a shared kiosk or tablet where many people
  answer on the same device, one after another.

Set this before you share so the limit matches how you're collecting.

## Sending it to a list instead

If you have a list of email addresses or phone numbers, use **Campaigns** to send
the survey directly and track opens and responses, rather than sharing a link
manually. See the Campaigns article for the walkthrough.
