# How do I invite teammates?

You can bring colleagues into your organization so they can build studies and
see your work. Invites and roles are managed from your team settings.

## Invite someone

Open your **Team** settings and send an invite to a colleague's email address.
They get a branded invite email with a link to join; if the email doesn't arrive,
you can copy the join link and send it yourself. When they open the link, they
see your organization name and their role, confirm their account, and they're
signed in — no separate second login.

Invite links expire after a week. If one lapses or you sent it to the wrong
place, you can **resend** or **revoke** pending invites from the same page.

## Owner vs. member — who can do what

Every person in your organization has one of two roles:

- **Owner** — full control of the organization. Owners can invite and remove
  members, change roles, and manage organization settings and branding.
- **Member** — can create and manage their own studies, agents, and analyses,
  but not organization-wide settings or the team roster.

You choose the role when you invite someone, and owners can adjust it later.

## Manage your team

From the **Team** page you can see everyone in the organization, change a
person's role between owner and member, and remove someone who no longer needs
access. Removing a member revokes their access to your organization right away.

## A note on plans and access

Which tools each person can use depends on the features enabled for your
organization. If a teammate can't see a product you expected them to have, that's
usually a plan/feature setting — reach out to your account team to adjust it.
