# How do I manage my organization and branding?

Your organization holds your team, your studies, and the branding that shows up
across the product. Organization-wide settings are managed by an **owner**.

## Organization settings

From your organization settings, an owner can update your **organization name**
and manage the **team** (invite, remove, and set roles). The organization name
appears in the app header so everyone knows which workspace they're in.

## Your logo

Upload your **logo** in organization settings and it's used across the product —
in the navigation, on campaign emails you send, and on the headers of analytics
reports and exports. Uploading it once applies it everywhere your organization's
branding appears, so your studies and reports look consistent.

## Study colors and branding

Colors and per-study branding are set on each study, not organization-wide. When
you build a survey, the **Basics** step lets you pick a bot name and emoji,
choose from built-in color presets or set your own colors, and add a custom
branding label you can show or hide. That way each study can match the audience
or campaign it's for.

For client-facing agents and exports, branding follows your chosen colors while
keeping the "powered by Datanautix" mark.

## Who can change what

- **Owners** manage organization name, logo, the team, and which tools are
  enabled.
- **Members** manage the branding on their own studies and agents.

If you need a product turned on for your organization, or aren't sure what your
plan includes, contact your account team.
