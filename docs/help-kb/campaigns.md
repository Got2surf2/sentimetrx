# How do I send a survey by email or SMS (Campaigns)?

**Campaigns** send a survey out to a list of people by email, SMS, or both, and
track who opened, clicked, and responded. Build your survey first, then create a
campaign linked to it.

## Set up the campaign

Create a campaign and link it to your study. Give it a name, an optional
description, and a target response goal. Choose the channel — **email**, **SMS**,
or **both**. SMS is only sent to recipients who have a phone number.

## Add recipients

Bulk upload a list from CSV, TSV, JSON, or Excel, or add people individually.
When you upload, map your columns to fields like first name, last name, email,
company, and any custom fields you want to use later. Sentimetrx validates emails
and flags duplicates for you.

## Build the email

The **Email Template Builder** has a visual block editor (with a raw-HTML mode if
you prefer). Stack blocks like paragraphs, headings, images, numbered steps,
buttons, dividers, and a signature, and reorder them freely. Pick a header layout,
set your subject line, and drop in **merge tags** — such as {{first_name}} or
{{survey_link}} — to personalize each message. A live preview shows how it looks
with sample values. Your primary button auto-links to the survey.

## Send or schedule

Send a test to yourself first. Then send to everyone at once, or schedule it for a
specific date and time. You can also add **reminder** emails that go out later —
targeted to everyone, non-responders only, or people who started but didn't finish.

## Track results

Watch campaign stats as they come in: sent count, open rate, click rate, and
completion rate, plus a per-recipient status (sent, opened, clicked, completed,
bounced, unsubscribed).

## Opt-outs are handled

Every email includes an automatic unsubscribe link, and anyone who unsubscribes is
excluded from future sends — you don't have to manage that yourself.
