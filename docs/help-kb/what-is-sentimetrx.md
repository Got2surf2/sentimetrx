# What is Sentimetrx?

Sentimetrx is a conversational survey and feedback-intelligence platform. You
collect feedback through chat-style surveys or AI agents, then turn the open-ended
answers into themes, entities, and statistics you can act on.

## The main tools, and when to use each

- **Surveys** — chat-style questionnaires your audience answers in a friendly,
  back-and-forth format. Start here to collect new feedback. Build one in the
  survey creator and share it by link or QR code.
- **Agents** — branded AI chatbots that hold a real conversation with your
  audience (answering questions, probing for detail). Use an agent when you want
  a two-way conversation rather than a fixed questionnaire.
- **Advanced Analytics** (the Analyze module) — where your responses become
  insight. It includes **TextMine** (themes, entities, dimensions, filtering),
  the **Statistics** tab (charts, key drivers), full-text **Search**, and
  **Ask Ana**.
- **Ask Ana** — an AI analyst that answers questions about *your data* ("what did
  people say about parking?"). It lives inside a dataset in Advanced Analytics.
- **PulseIQ** — run a live, digital pulse/poll session and see responses come in
  in real time.
- **Town Hall** — record an in-person meeting and turn the transcript into a
  clean Q&A report.
- **Campaigns** — send a survey out by email or SMS and track opens and responses.

## Getting help vs. asking about your data

There are two assistants, and they do different jobs:

- **Help** — click the 🛟 lifesaver in the bottom-right corner of any page to chat with **Sherpa**,
  who answers *how to use Sentimetrx* ("how do I export a deck?", "what's a Super
  Agent?").
- **Ask Ana** (inside a dataset) answers *what your data says*.

If you ask Sherpa about your data, it'll point you to Ask Ana. If you ask Ana how to
navigate the product, it'll point you back to Help.
