# How do I search my responses?

When you want to jump straight to specific comments rather than browse themes,
**Search** finds them fast. It's part of **TextMine** in Analytics — open
the search box on your dataset and type what you're looking for.

## Plain keyword search

Type a word or phrase and Search returns the responses that contain it, ranked by
relevance, with your term highlighted. This is the quick way to answer "who
mentioned parking?" or "did anyone say refund?" — exact, fast, and literal.

## AI-augmented search

Turn on **AI Search** for a smarter pass. Instead of matching only the exact words
you typed, it expands your query to related terms and ranks results by how well each
comment actually answers what you asked. So a search for "disappointed with the
food" also surfaces "the meal was a letdown," even though none of those words match
literally. Use it when you care about a concept or feeling, not a specific keyword.

- **Plain search** — best when you know the exact word or name you want.
- **AI search** — best when you want the *idea*, phrased however people said it.

## Search across a whole collection

If you've grouped datasets into a **collection**, Search works across all of them at
once, so you can find every mention of a topic spanning multiple studies or sources
without searching each dataset one by one.

## Search vs. asking a question

Search hands you the matching comments to read yourself. If you'd rather get a
written answer — "summarize what unhappy diners said about wait times" — use **Ask
Ana** inside the dataset instead. Search finds the quotes; Ask Ana explains them.
