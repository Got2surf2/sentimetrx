# How do I add data?

Everything you analyze in **Analytics** lives in a **dataset**. There are
a few ways to get one — import a file, upload a spreadsheet, or connect an outside
source — depending on where your feedback comes from. Start a new one from
**Analytics** and pick the source.

## From your survey responses

If you've collected feedback with a Sentimetrx **survey**, those responses can flow
straight into a dataset — no export or re-upload. Choose the survey as your source
and Sentimetrx brings the responses in and sets up the fields for you, so you can
go straight to themes and charts.

## Upload a CSV

Have data from somewhere else — a spreadsheet, another tool, an export? Upload it as
a **CSV**. Sentimetrx reads your columns and **auto-detects a type** for each one
(open-ended, categorical, numeric, date, ID). After the upload, open the **Schema**
tab and confirm those guesses — especially that your comment columns are marked
**open-ended**, since that's what TextMine mines for themes.

## Pull in outside feedback sources

Beyond surveys and CSVs, Sentimetrx can pull feedback in from several external
sources so you can analyze it the same way. Depending on what's enabled for your
organization, these include:

- **Google reviews** for a business location
- **Reddit** discussions
- **Substack** comments
- Public regulatory comment dockets

Each source has a short wizard that walks you through choosing exactly what to bring
in (which location, thread, or post), then creates the dataset for you. Google
reviews can also keep syncing on a schedule so new reviews show up automatically.

## After the data lands

However it arrives, every dataset flows into the same analysis tools — TextMine,
the Statistics tab, Search, and **Ask Ana**. Do a quick **Schema** check first, then
mine your themes. If you want to know what the data *says* rather than how to load
it, ask **Ask Ana** inside the dataset.
