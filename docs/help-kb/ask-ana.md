# What is Ask Ana and how do I use it?

**Ask Ana** is an AI analyst that lives inside a dataset in **Advanced
Analytics**. Instead of building a chart or filter yourself, you just ask a
question about your feedback in plain language and Ana answers from the same data
your dataset holds.

## Ask a question about your data

Open a dataset in **Advanced Analytics** and go to **Ask Ana**. Type a question
the way you'd ask a colleague, for example:

- "What did detractors say about wait times?"
- "What are the top complaints about parking?"
- "How did people describe the staff?"

Ana reads your responses and answers in words, pulling from the actual comments
rather than making things up. If you have filters active in the view, Ana answers
for that segment.

## Get an ad-hoc report

When you want more than a single answer, Ana can produce an **ad-hoc report** — a
short, written summary grounded in your data that you can save as HTML or PDF.
Describe what you want the report to cover and Ana drafts it from the same
responses, honoring the filters you have applied.

## Ask Ana vs. Help — which one?

There are two assistants and they answer different kinds of questions:

- **Ask Ana** answers **what your data says** — the content of your feedback. It
  lives inside a dataset.
- **Help** (the 🛟 lifesaver at the top of any page, where you chat with **Guide**)
  answers **how to use Sentimetrx** — where to click, how a feature works, how to
  export a deck.

If you ask Ana how to navigate the product, it'll point you to Help. If you ask
Guide what your data says, it'll point you back to Ask Ana. Pick Ana for
insight about your responses; pick Help for how-to.
