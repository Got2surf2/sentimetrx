# How do I export a report or deck?

When you're ready to share findings, Analytics can turn your dataset into a
deck, a report, or a data file — with charts and summaries already built. You'll
find the export options on your dataset in **Analytics**.

## Exports reflect your active filters

Whatever **Filters** are active when you export is what the export contains. If
you've narrowed to detractors, or one region, or a date range, the deck and report
reflect exactly that segment. Clear your filters first if you want the full dataset,
or set them deliberately to export a focused cut.

## PowerPoint (PPTX) deck

Export a **PowerPoint** deck with your theme charts, segment comparisons, key
insights, and a methodology note. It's editable in PowerPoint, so you can drop
slides into a larger presentation or tweak wording before sharing.

## PDF / HTML report

Prefer a report? The **interactive HTML report** embeds your charts and reads
cleanly on screen. You can also produce a shareable version to hand to someone who
isn't in your Sentimetrx account.

## Shareable link

Generate a **shareable link** to give someone read-only access to the report
without a login — handy for a stakeholder who just needs to see the results.

## Filtered CSV

To keep working with the raw numbers, export the underlying rows as a **CSV**. Like
every other export, it honors your active filters, so you get exactly the segment
you're viewing.

## Which should I use?

Use the **PPTX deck** for a presentation, the **HTML report** or **shareable link**
for a quick read-only share, and the **CSV** when someone needs the data itself. If
you instead want a written answer to a specific question about the data, ask **Ask
Ana** inside the dataset.
