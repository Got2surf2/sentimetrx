# How do I group datasets (Collections & Brands)?

Most analysis happens inside one dataset, but sometimes you want to look across
several at once — multiple locations, several surveys, or feedback from different
sources about the same thing. That's what **collections** are for.

## Collections

A **collection** groups multiple datasets so you can analyze them together as if
they were one. Themes, charts, filters, and Search all span every dataset in the
collection, so you can compare and roll up across studies instead of opening each
one separately.

Use a collection when you want to:

- Compare feedback across **locations, regions, or stores**.
- Combine **several surveys** on the same topic into one view.
- Analyze feedback from **different sources** — say survey responses alongside
  Google reviews — side by side.

## Brand collections

A **brand collection** is a collection that groups itself automatically. When your
datasets carry a **brand tag**, Sentimetrx gathers all of a brand's datasets under a
single **Brand** card, so you can drill in to any member dataset or study the brand
as a whole — without hand-picking which datasets belong. It's the low-effort way to
keep a brand's feedback together as new datasets are added.

## Collection or single dataset?

Stay in a **single dataset** for the cleanest, most focused view of one study. Reach
for a **collection** the moment you need cross-dataset comparison or a combined
total. And when you want a written answer that spans the group, **Ask Ana** works
inside a collection too — just ask it there.
