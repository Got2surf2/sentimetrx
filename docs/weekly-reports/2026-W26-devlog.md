### Outlet Action-Plan predictor (themes, de-biased v2) — 2026-06-22

**Why**: The Leaderboard (above) ranks outlets per theme but doesn't tell an operator *what to do*. Owner wants the per-outlet report "much richer and actionable" — the highest-impact things to work on relative to peers, with examples of what to fix and **who to learn from** — and a brand-level executive summary that becomes the "why you need us" guest-experience improvement plan. Chose **themes over dimensions** (the 7-axis keyword taxonomy folds every service/food complaint into "Experience" → can't answer "lift Service to median"; themes separate cleanly) and **de-biased v2 over naive v1** (owner call).

**What changed**:
- **`lib/outletPredictor.ts` (new, pure/unit-tested)** — `buildPredictor()`. **v2 model**: multivariate **ridge regression** of star rating on per-review theme-negative dummies; `drag(T) = −β_T` is the de-biased ★-drag. Ridge (L2, intercept unpenalized, **λ by 5-fold CV**) splits credit across **co-occurring** complaints that the naive univariate severity double-counts. **Ridge-linear not logistic on purpose** — outcome is continuous 1–5★ and the what-if is ★-denominated, so β_T *is* the ★-drag. Outputs: `themeDrag` (v1 vs v2 + shrink%), `brandLevers` (vol-weighted chain ★ if all→median), per-outlet `outletLevers` (gap×drag, exemplar peer, example quote). **Exemplar fix**: mentor = lowest neg-rate among outlets with ≥60 reviews, tie-broken by ★ then volume (a volume floor stops tiny-outlet fluke 0%s from winning).
- **`lib/outletReport.ts`** — `scanDataset` now also captures `reviewMatrix` (per rated text-review `{placeId, rating, neg[]}`) + `themeLabels` inside the *existing* flat-row loop (whole-review polarity, identical to the leaderboard → engine ≡ leaderboard). Added `computeOutletPredictor` and `computeOutletReportWithPredictor` (report + predictor from ONE scan).
- **UI** — new **Action Plan** tab in `OutletReportTabs.tsx` (now the **default** tab), `ActionPlan`/`LeverCard`: ranked levers with projected +★, guest quote ("what to fix"), and an emerald "Learn from <peer>" call-out. Empty state when the outlet is at/above peer median on every theme. Footnote explains the de-biased model + R² + the associational caveat. `page.tsx` switched to `computeOutletReportWithPredictor`.
- **`tests/unit/outletPredictor.test.ts`** — synthetic-data unit tests: recovers known per-theme drag + ranking, de-biases co-occurrence (v2 < v1, shrink > 0), attaches quotes, picks the cleaner outlet as exemplar.

**Validated on real data** (Rubio's, 5,754 rated text-reviews, 4.44★, 71 outlets ≥30): production `computeOutletPredictor` matches the standalone prototype exactly — λ=3, R² 0.225, **Staff & Customer Service** is the #1 lever and *shrinks least under de-biasing (28%)* → its drag is genuinely its own, not borrowed from globally-bad reviews; Value/Pricing shrank 70% (mostly co-occurrence illusion).
